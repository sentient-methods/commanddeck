## 3. Core Historical Copy & Lore Preserved

### Arena Identity & Origins
- **Location**: Provo Towne Centre, Provo, Utah (ground floor below Cinemark 16, right next to the children's play area).
- **Core Concept**: Close Quarters Battle (CQB) Academy laser tag. Designed for non-stop action, rapid maneuvering, and small tactical squad battles (2 to 8 players) with dedicated live referee supervision.
- **Technology & Features**:
  - High-tech blasters displaying real-time ammunition count and health points on the weapon HUD.
  - Infrared beam emitters paired with ballistic eye protection or full face masks.
  - RFID base stations for ammo caches, medic/respawn points, and objective flags.
  - Real-time digital telemetry scoreboards inside and outside the arena with live video observation feeds for spectators and waiting parents.
  - Dynamic arena reactive lighting effects and tempo-accelerating soundtracks during matches.
  - Industrial foam-padded walls with impact-absorbing swayed barriers featuring pop-out crush zones for player collision protection.
