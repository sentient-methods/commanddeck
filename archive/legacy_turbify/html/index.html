Founded as a family friendly arena at the Provo Towne Centre, Command Deck has now gone mobile,
taking the modern, clean, safe, and high-tech experience that thousands have come to love on the road.
Our cutting edge equipment displays ammo and health-points and features RFID technology for scanning
ammo dumps and re-spawn bases, and we can bring all of it, including scoreboards with real-time scores
and game stats to any location indoors or outdoors, night or day.
Originally themed to target children ages 12 and under, Command Deck has evolved from a family-friendly
arena to a mobile entertainment experience, perfect for everything from birthday parties to bachelor
parties, group dates, family reunions, or intense tactical training for players of any age.
